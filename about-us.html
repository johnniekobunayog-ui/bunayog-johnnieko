About Us

Get To’s Thrift was inspired by the name “Getto Street,” which gives off a simple, urban, and street-style vibe. We chose this name because we wanted our thrift shop to have a fun and unique identity that represents affordable fashion and the experience of finding good clothes at a low price.

Get To’s Thrift is a thrift shop that offers affordable, stylish, and unique clothing for people who want to express their own style without spending too much. We believe that looking good does not always have to be expensive. Sometimes, you just need to explore and find the right piece.

More than just selling clothes, we want Get To’s Thrift to be a place where people can discover different styles, find something that fits their personality, and enjoy the simple experience of thrift shopping.

We also believe that choosing thrifted clothes can encourage people to reuse clothing and make more practical fashion choices.

Get To’s Thrift — affordable finds, unique style, and a little bit of street vibe.
